6. Method and limits

Remove padded audio centers; separately standardize native curves; interpolate to shared 25 Hz support; apply centered Hann-31 smoothing; trim two seconds at both ends; shift music by −2, −1, −0.5, 0, +0.5, +1 or +2 seconds. Half-second offsets use linear interpolation at 12.5 samples. No wrap, extrapolation or shift-specific normalization. Each clip retains at least 125 anchors.

Mean absolute difference = mean over anchors of |video(t) − music(t + lag)|. Median absolute difference replaces that mean with a median. Fixed-support zero is not the older full-support zero analysis. The original 57 OOM cases, review rows and two prior near-constant shift failures remain outside this cohort.

The cluster script reconstructed all seven prior Pearson scores and anchor counts; downloaded per-clip errors are zero. Local checks validate saved numerical outputs and provenance, not raw-curve or media contents. No significance test or confidence interval is reported. Movie clustering, interpolation and smoothing should be considered when interpreting the small changes.
