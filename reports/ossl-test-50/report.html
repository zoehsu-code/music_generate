OSSL Video and Paired Music: Event Curve Analysis

Both curves are independently standardized and aligned to a common 25 Hz time grid, then smoothed with a 31-point symmetric Hann window (1.2-second endpoint span). The primary score is the zero-lag Pearson correlation. Blue: video; orange: music.

High and low denote the top and bottom 20% of eligible samples in this batch, not absolute quality labels. Paired clips are assumed to share the same start time; no music shift is applied when computing the primary score. Positive diagnostic lag means music changes occur later. Lag correlations use a fixed, cropped time interval and should not be compared directly with the primary full-interval score.
